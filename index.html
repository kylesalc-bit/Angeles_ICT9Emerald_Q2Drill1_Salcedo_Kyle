Beginner's Guide to Basketball

Fundementals

Basketball is full of fundementals. Before starting, you must learn the fundementals in order to understand and play. Here are what needed on fundementals: Dribbling, Shooting, and Play Decision making.

Physical Condition and Movement

In basketball, you are required to have great physical body being able to handle your stamina and mind and also needed great footwork. Working with these enhances you movement in both offense and defense.

Thinking skills

Thinking is also required in basketball, strategizing helps the team in offense and defense like plays and scoring.Understanding and reading a play can lead to success in defeating a team. Create a a good play like spacing out others for wide open shots requires thinking too.
